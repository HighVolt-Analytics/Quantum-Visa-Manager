01 Visa operations

The administration between engagement and lodgement, handled.

Requests, reminders, checks, filing, checklists, forms, timelines and estimates — running in the background of every matter.

  • Customisable pipelines from onboarding to visa outcome, shaped to your practice.
  • Stage, owner, due dates and blockers tracked on every matter.
  • Fee and timeline estimators clearly labelled as estimates.

02 AI correspondence and tasking

Every email becomes the right next action.

Quantum reads authorised inbound email from clients and the Department, understands what is being asked in the chain, creates an internal task, assigns the responsible person and drafts a response.

  • Gmail and Outlook connections for practice mailboxes.
  • Proposed versus verified deadlines. Only the practitioner-verified date governs the task.
  • Drafts wait for approval before anything is sent.

03 Document and checklist automation

Checked, filed and ticked off — before you open it.

Quantum understands the document requirements of each visa stream and every incoming attachment. It identifies what has arrived and what is outstanding, assesses expiry and suitability, files to the right matter folder, updates the checklist and routes for your final review.

  • Checklists generated from the visa stream and the client’s circumstances, regenerated when answers change.
  • Customisable document descriptions so clients know exactly what to send.
  • Exceptions, not guesses when a document doesn’t meet the requirement.
04 Forms and prefill

Type it once. Verify it once.

After you accept a document, its verified details map to the applicable forms for the visa stream. A browser extension prefills matching fields on government web forms for you to check.

Assisted preparation, not unattended lodgement. Every prefilled entry is checked and verified by the consultant. Field coverage varies by form, and declarations, submission and lodgement always remain with you.
05 Living visa library

Requirements change. Your references keep up.

Quantum monitors government publications for changes to visa rules, forms, timelines, fees and documentary requirements, identifies what they affect, and proposes updates for approval.

  • Source-linked proposals with effective dates and a before-and-after view.
  • Approved once, applied everywhere — templates, checklists, estimators and library pages.
  • Lodged matters keep their history. Changes never rewrite the past.
