Post-lodgement

Department and client emails, turned into tasks.

After lodgement, correspondence volume rises and important requests are easy to miss. Quantum reads each authorised email, interprets the request in the chain, creates the task, assigns the owner and drafts the response.

  • Triggered by receipt and understanding of correspondence — not only by rule changes.
  • Proposed dates are shown alongside the practitioner-verified legal deadline, which alone governs.
  • Milestone updates to clients go out only in the forms you’ve approved.
Regulatory library

Government changes become approved updates.

Changes to visa rules, forms, timelines, fees and documentary requirements are detected, assessed for impact and proposed for your approval. Once approved, they flow through checklists, templates, estimators and the visa library your consultants use.
