Can I turn AI off for specific tasks?

Yes. The AI Control Matrix lets you set each supported action to Automate, Review first or Manual. Some actions — such as accepting a document, setting a governing deadline or lodging — are always performed by a person.

Who approves forms?

Your consultant. Quantum maps verified details into forms and can prefill government web-form fields through a browser extension, but every entry is checked and verified by the consultant. Quantum does not submit or lodge applications.

How are Department updates handled?

Two ways. Correspondence from the Department on a matter becomes a task with an owner, a proposed date and a draft response; the practitioner verifies the legal deadline. Published changes to rules, forms, fees or benchmarks become proposals for your visa library, applied only after approval.

Where is data hosted?

Australian data residency is available, and hosting is set per practice tenant. Where another supported country is required, it is confirmed in your agreement, together with the boundaries for storage, backups, AI processing and subprocessors.

Can I export logs and backups?

Yes. Administrators can review and download the audit log — covering both AI actions and human approvals — and a monthly backup can be sent to a supported drive your practice selects.

How do I migrate from my current system?

Through a guided three-step process: assess and export, map and validate, then import and verify your active matters. We confirm the supported source systems, formats and historical records during assessment, before you commit.

Does the software provide migration advice?

No. Quantum Visa Manager is administrative software. Immigration assistance must be given by a registered migration agent or another person permitted under the Migration Act 1958. The AI receptionist answers general service questions only and hands over to your consultant.
