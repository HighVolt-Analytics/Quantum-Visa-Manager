Onboarding and migration

Switching software shouldn’t mean starting again.

A typical sequence, agreed with you before we begin. Timing depends on your data and the number of active matters.

Phase 1

Discover and configure

Visa streams, pipelines, checklists, templates, naming, SLAs and AI controls set to your practice.

Phase 2

Assess and map

We confirm what your current system can export, map fields to Quantum and share a validation report.

Phase 3

Import and verify

Active matters are imported and reconciled — record counts, documents and key dates — before cut-over.

Phase 4

Train and go live

Role-based training for consultants and staff, then a supported go-live period.

What we confirm before you commit: supported source systems and export formats, which documents and historical records can move, field mapping, data-integrity checks and the cut-over plan. We don’t promise universal zero-downtime migration.
