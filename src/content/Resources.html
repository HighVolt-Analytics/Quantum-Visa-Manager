FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does Quantum lodge applications?

No. Quantum prepares forms and can prefill government web-form fields for review. Declarations, submission and lodgement are always performed by the consultant.

Can clients upload documents directly?

Clients can email documents to the connected practice mailbox or upload them through the client portal. Either way, Quantum checks, files and updates the checklist.

What happens when Quantum isn’t sure?

It raises an exception and routes the item to a person. It doesn’t guess, and it doesn’t file unsuitable documents as accepted.

Which visa subclasses are supported?

Checklists, forms and timelines are configured per visa stream in your visa library. We confirm the subclasses and streams your practice needs during onboarding.

Does the AI receptionist give advice?

No. It answers general questions about your services, captures details and books consultations. Immigration assistance is given only by your registered migration agents.

Can we use our own templates?

Yes. Requests, reminders, service agreements, invoices and client messages use your templates and wording.

Security and compliance

Plain-English explanations.

How Quantum supports — but never replaces — your professional and privacy obligations.

Administrative assistance vs professional judgment

Quantum performs administrative tasks. Advice, document acceptance, approvals, deadlines and lodgement remain with registered migration agents.

Privacy

Built to support your obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles, with role-based access and masked imagery.

Data residency

Australian residency is available. Other supported countries are confirmed per tenant, including storage, backup and AI-processing boundaries.

Audit and record keeping

AI actions and human approvals are logged together. Administrators can review and download logs.

Backups and exit

Monthly backups to your chosen supported destination, and a full export if you ever leave.

No guarantees of outcomes

Calculators and timelines are estimates. Quantum never promises visa outcomes or processing times.
