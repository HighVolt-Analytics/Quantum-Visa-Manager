02 Access control

Five checks before anyone sees a file.

Knowing where a document is stored is never enough to open it. Every view or download passes each gate in turn.

01

Who are you?

Password stored as a bcrypt hash, plus a one-time email code.

02

What’s your role?

Client, consultant, practice admin or platform admin.

03

Which practice?

Organisation context from your token, enforced again in the database.

04

Is this record yours?

Consultants see only the clients assigned to them.

05

Deliver securely

Streamed through the API or a short-lived signed link — and logged.
