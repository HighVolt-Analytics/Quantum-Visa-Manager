Privacy Policy

Draft for legal review · Last updated 14 October 2026

High Volt Analytics (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information in connection with the Quantum Visa Manager website and platform, in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Two roles

When you visit our website or request a demonstration, we collect and handle your personal information directly. When a migration practice uses Quantum Visa Manager, the practice decides what client information is processed, and we handle that information on the practice’s behalf under our agreement with them.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, work email, phone and practice name.
  • Information about your practice that you choose to share, such as size, current system and priorities.
  • Technical information about how our website is used.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a demonstration.
  • To provide, support and improve the platform for our customers.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

Storage and residency

Platform data is stored in the hosting region selected for each customer tenant. Australian data residency is available. Details of storage, backups, AI processing and subprocessors are set out in our customer agreements.

Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose it to service providers who help us operate, under confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.

Access and correction

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you. If you are a client of a migration practice, please contact that practice first.

Complaints

Contact privacy@highvolt.tech. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
