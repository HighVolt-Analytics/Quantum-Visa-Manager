Pricing FAQs

Questions, answered.

Are prices shown in Australian dollars?

Yes. All quotes are in AUD and exclude GST, which is added where applicable.

Is there a minimum term?

Your quote sets out the term, notice period and what happens to your data if you leave — including a full export.

How is AI usage charged?

Each package includes an AI usage allowance. Higher volumes can be added at the rate set out in your quote. You can also set actions to Manual in the AI Control Matrix to manage usage.

Is data migration included?

Migration is scoped after we assess your current system, and the cost is set out separately in your quote.

Can we start with one team or office?

Yes. You can start with a single team or visa stream and expand once the workflow is proven.
