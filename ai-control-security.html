01 AI Control Matrix

Switch AI on, off, or to review — action by action.

Every supported action can be set to Automate, Review first or Manual. Try it: changes are counted and logged, just as they are in the product.

Some actions are always human. Accepting a document as evidence, setting a governing legal deadline and lodging an application cannot be automated.

03 Configuration

Your workflows, not ours.

Pipelines, stages, checklists, naming conventions, reminder cadence, templates and internal SLAs are all configured by your practice. AI behaviour is set through configuration — it doesn’t quietly learn new habits.

  • Exception handoffs route unclear requests, unsuitable documents and low-confidence results to a person.
  • Matter-level overrides for sensitive or complex files.

06 Security

What is stored, processed, retained and exported.

Plain answers, so your practice can meet its privacy and record-keeping obligations.

AreaWhat it means
StoredClient and matter records, documents, the communications you connect, settings and the audit log — in your tenant’s hosting region.
ProcessedAI reads only the email accounts and documents you authorise, to classify, check, extract and draft. Processing locations, including AI inference and subprocessors, are documented in your agreement.
RetainedAccording to your practice’s retention settings and your record-keeping obligations as a migration practice.
ExportedAudit logs on demand, monthly backups to your selected destination, and a full data export on request or exit.
AccessRole-based permissions for practice owners, registered migration agents, case officers and scoped sponsor HR users.
PrivacyDesigned to support your obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Personal information is masked in all public product imagery.
